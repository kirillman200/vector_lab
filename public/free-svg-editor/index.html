How it works

From file to finished vector in three steps.

  1. Open or paste an SVG. Use your own file, paste SVG source, or begin with the included sample.
  2. Edit visually or precisely. Drag vectors and path handles, then fine-tune geometry, paint, transforms, and raw attributes.
  3. Export a standard file. Download SVG or PNG without converting your work into a proprietary project format.

What “free” means

No trial and no account wall.

The editor is available without payment or registration. Advertising may support the hosted site, but the editing tools are not placed behind a paid tier.

The source code is published under the MIT License, so it can be inspected, adapted, and run independently under that license.

Privacy by design

Your artwork remains on your device.

SVG Vector Lab performs its editing work in the browser. Files are not intentionally uploaded to an application server. Work in progress may be autosaved in your browser's local storage so it can be restored on your next visit.

Hosting, analytics, consent, and advertising services may still receive ordinary website-request information. The Privacy Policy explains those services and your choices.

Common questions

Free SVG editor FAQ

Is SVG Vector Lab really free?

Yes. The hosted editor is free to use and requires no account. The project source is also available under the MIT License.

Does it upload my artwork?

No. Artwork is opened, edited, autosaved, and exported in your browser. It is not intentionally uploaded to an application server.

Can I edit individual path points?

Yes. Drag endpoints and Bezier control points directly, or enter exact path-command values in the Path panel.

Can it export PNG?

Yes. Export the current drawing as SVG or render it as PNG at several resolution multipliers.
