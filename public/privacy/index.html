Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information SVG Vector Lab handles, what stays on your device, and what third-party services may receive when the site is hosted or monetized.

Effective: July 29, 2026

Your SVG artwork is processed locally. SVG Vector Lab does not upload the files you open, the SVG markup you edit, or the images you export to an application server.

Information stored on your device

The editor uses your browser's local storage to retain the current work in progress, a manual checkpoint, and your analytics choice between visits. This information remains in that browser profile unless you clear site data, use private browsing, or your browser removes it. You can remove it through your browser's site-data controls.

The editor also uses standard browser capabilities when you choose to open a local SVG file, copy SVG markup, or download an SVG or PNG. Those actions are initiated by you.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting and content-delivery provider may automatically process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site. Depending on the provider, this can include IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps, referring page, and security events. The provider handles that information under its own privacy terms.

Analytics

With your permission, SVG Vector Lab uses Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic and broad editor workflows. The Google tag is blocked until you choose Allow analytics. If you allow it, Google Analytics may set analytics cookies and receive information such as the visited path, page title, referring origin, approximate location, browser, device, operating system, and interaction events.

Site and editor events use fixed, low-cardinality labels for navigation destinations, controls, tabs, document source, selected tool, general element type, grouping, alignment, imports, and SVG, PNG, or clipboard exports. The site may also record whether a named ad placement was filled, unfilled, or visibly on screen. Analytics never receives SVG contents, source-panel text, attribute values, filenames, clipboard contents, image data, raw pointer coordinates, or keystrokes. Google Analytics is configured without Google Signals or ad-personalization signals.

Ad clicks, impressions, and publisher revenue are measured by Google AdSense and may appear in Google Analytics through the official account link. SVG Vector Lab does not intercept clicks inside ads or place tracking overlays over ad units.

You can change this choice at any time with the Analytics choices button. Global Privacy Control is honored as an analytics opt-out. The site stores the choice in local storage. Analytics data is used to improve the product and is not used by SVG Vector Lab to create individual user profiles.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertising and cookies

The site uses Google AdSense to request and display advertising when Google has approved the site and an eligible ad is available. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google's advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site or other sites.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, the site will present a consent message before using advertising cookies or processing personal data for personalized advertising. Visitors may be offered personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no advertising request depending on their choices and region.

Information you send voluntarily

If you contact the project through GitHub, GitHub processes your account information and message under its own privacy statement. Do not include confidential SVG files or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Children's privacy

SVG Vector Lab is a general-purpose design and development tool and is not directed to children under 13. The project does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear SVG Vector Lab's local storage through your browser settings.
  • Block or delete cookies using your browser controls.
  • Use the Analytics choices control to allow or disable Google Analytics.
  • Manage personalized Google advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • Avoid submitting personal information through public GitHub issues.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features, hosting, analytics, advertising providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@svgvectorlab.com. Do not attach confidential artwork or access credentials.
